Teenage reliquary. Strange to look back at this thing I made as a sophomore and see how weirdly well its aesthetic qualities suit the stuff I've made this year!! Life is cyclical! Reflecting on being so sad as an adolescent and not being able to imagine myself at age 23 graduating with a BFA in Studio Arts. I've grown so much since then and there is still a lot of growing and changing and healing and self-bettering for me to do. I know that there is a future a few years down the road that I cannot now even begin to imagine and I am just so excited to live it.
